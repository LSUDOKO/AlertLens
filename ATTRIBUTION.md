# Attribution

## Data
All alerts, users, hosts, cases and reputation lists are synthetic, generated by `data/generator/`. Addresses come from reserved documentation ranges (RFC 5737) and `10.0.0.0/8`, domains from `example.com` and `example.org` (RFC 2606), users end in `.test`. No real logs, people or systems were used.

## MITRE ATT&CK
Technique IDs, names and tactics in `alertlens/mitre/attack_subset.json` come from MITRE ATT&CK®. Short descriptions, detection tips and the transition table are our own.

## Research
Design inspiration, not reproductions:
- Pandey and Bhujang, *Poisoning the Watchtower: Prompt Injection Attacks Against LLM-Augmented Security Operations Through Adversarial Log Content*, arXiv:2605.24421. Its persona and context payload styles were added to our payload list and detector.
- Dai and Guerra-Manzanares, *StateFence: Task-State Protection Against Evidence-Chain Contamination in Blue-Team LLM Agents*, arXiv:2611.02605. Inspired the task-state lock.
